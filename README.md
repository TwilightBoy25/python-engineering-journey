## Engineering Philosophy

> "Remain Uncomfortable."

I believe growth comes from consistently challenging the limits of what I currently understand.

Programming is not about memorizing every function or knowing every answer immediately. It's about learning how to approach unfamiliar problems, break them into manageable components, experiment, and develop effective solutions.

Every project is an opportunity to become a better engineer.

**Consistency over intensity. Understanding over memorization. Progress over perfection.**
